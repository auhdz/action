# Acción — Funding Targets: Angels, Accelerators & Grants

> Non-VC first. These are the right entry points before institutional capital.
> Updated: April 2026. Verify all contact info before outreach — it changes.

---

## Prioritization Framework

**Highest probability of saying yes:**
1. Orgs/people with personal proximity to the problem (Latino, immigrant family, LA-based)
2. Orgs/people who have funded community safety or civil liberties tech before
3. Programs that explicitly target underrepresented founders

**Do in this order:**
1. Non-dilutive grants first (no equity cost)
2. Accelerators second (structure + network + small check)
3. Latino/impact angels third (relationship-dependent, takes time)
4. Institutional VCs last (once you have traction)

## SECTION 1: ACCELERATORS

These programs are the fastest path to your first check + network. Apply to all of them.

---

### Manos Accelerator
**What they do:** 4-month accelerator for Latino entrepreneurs. Cohort-based, intensive mentorship, community network.
**Equity/check:** Varies by cohort — typically $25K–$75K for 5–8% equity, or fellowship formats
**Why they're right for Acción:** Explicitly serve Latino founders. Strong network in LA and Silicon Valley. Alumni include successful Latino-led tech companies.
**Website:** manosaccelerator.com
**Apply:** applications open periodically — check site for current cycle
**How to build the connection today:**
- Follow @ManosAccelerator on LinkedIn and Twitter
- Tag them when you post about building Acción
- Reach out to alumni on LinkedIn — "I'm applying, would love 15 min to learn about your experience"
- Attend any Manos community events (they host regularly in SV and LA)

## SECTION 3: ANGELS & INDIVIDUAL INVESTORS

**These relationships take longer but can move faster than institutional rounds. One warm intro > 100 cold emails.**

---

### Strategy for Finding Latino Angels in LA

**LinkedIn approach:**
Search: "Mexican American" OR "Latino" AND "founder" AND "Los Angeles" AND "angel investor"
Filter to 2nd-degree connections. Ask mutual connections for warm intros.

**Twitter/X approach:**
Follow #LatinoFounders #TechnoLatino hashtags. The community is active and warm.

**Events to attend in LA:**
- Latino Startup Alliance events (latinostartupalliance.com)
- Manos Accelerator alumni events
- HACLA (Hispanic Alliance for Career Enhancement) LA chapter events
- Any Techstars LA events
- Latinx in AI / Latinx Tech Collective events

---

### Target Profiles to Search For

**Profile 1: Latino tech founder who has had an exit**
These people have capital, proximity to the problem, and usually want to give back.
Search for: Latino founders who raised VC, sold companies, or went through acquisitions in 2015–2023.

**Profile 2: Latino entertainment/media industry executive in LA**
Hollywood has Latino executives with capital who care deeply about community representation.
Think: Senior roles at Univision, Telemundo, Netflix Latino content, studios.

**Profile 3: Immigration attorney turned investor**
They see the problem every day. They have networks. Many have made angel investments in the space.

**Profile 4: LA-based community health or nonprofit executive who also invests**
Cross-sector people who understand the community AND capital.

That email is 3 sentences. It respects their time. It's honest about where you are. It almost always gets a reply.
